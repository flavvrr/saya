the game

  
artists search "type beat." your titles and tags do the selling.

  

    

      
Title formula

      
Free tag + artist type beat + your beat name. Use the artists the beat actually sounds like.

      
[FREE] Playboi Carti x Destroy Lonely Type Beat "NIGHTCRAWLER" (prod. Saya)

      
[FREE] Ken Carson x Homixide Gang Type Beat "VAMP SEASON" (prod. Saya)

      
"Free" means free for non-profit use with your tag on it. Anyone releasing it commercially buys a lease.
